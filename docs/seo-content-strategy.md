# SEO Content Strategy

## Scope

This strategy is based on the active services currently stored in Supabase. It prioritizes services that have clear user intent, recurring/seasonal demand, strong local-service conversion potential, and a direct path from an informational article to an existing service page.

No search-volume numbers are asserted here; priorities are based on intent, current relevance, service fit, and observed search/news activity. Search Console data should be used later to refine the order using actual impressions and queries.

## Recommended article architecture

Each high-intent service should initially receive one pillar article rather than many thin pages.

Example:

1. H1: ثبت نام سایپا ۱۴۰۵؛ شرایط، مدارک و نحوه ثبت نام
2. مقدمه کوتاه و current-status note
3. شرایط ثبت نام
4. مدارک و اطلاعات مورد نیاز
5. مراحل ثبت نام
6. زمان‌بندی و وضعیت آخرین طرح (with date and official-source reference)
7. خطاها و نکات مهم
8. FAQ
9. CTA: ثبت درخواست ثبت‌نام در کافی‌نت توسن
10. Related articles

The article must clearly distinguish current information from evergreen guidance. Time-sensitive details such as dates, product lists, prices, eligibility rules, and registration windows must carry a publication/update date and be refreshed when the official notice changes.

## Internal linking rules

- Every pillar article links to its target service page at least once near the beginning and once in the final CTA.
- Service pages should link back to the most relevant informational article(s) where the UI supports it.
- Related articles should link to the pillar article to create a topic cluster.
- Avoid keyword-stuffed anchor text; use natural Persian anchors such as «ثبت درخواست ثبت‌نام سایپا».
- Do not create multiple articles targeting the exact same intent unless there is a genuine search-intent difference.

## Freshness policy

For time-sensitive topics, never hard-code old dates as permanent SEO copy. Keep the article evergreen where possible and put changing information in a clearly dated section. Update the article when an official notice changes.

## Measurement plan

After publishing the first articles:

- Use Google Search Console to collect queries, impressions, CTR, average position, and indexed status.
- Compare article-to-service click-through and completed orders where analytics data is available.
- Expand clusters only when Search Console reveals meaningful related queries or the service has proven conversion value.
- Avoid judging SEO success immediately after publication; use trend data over time.
